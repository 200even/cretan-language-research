**Status:** completed against the rule registered in `experiments/mi-pa-sa-comparative-audit.md`.

The registered rule required both independent stem families for a suffix to survive at Tier A/B. No candidate satisfies that rule.

## Strongest positive: `I-DA ~ I-DA-MI`

This is the clean new local morphology result.

PK Za 18 and SY Za 1 are both libation tables. Their local structures are:

```text
PK Za 18   • I-DA    • JA-...
SY Za 1    • I-DA-MI • JA-...
```

The base and extended form are complete, cross-site, in the same ritual support/genre, and occupy an unusually similar immediate formulaic slot. The relationship is therefore promoted to **Tier A formal morphology**. No grammatical or semantic value is assigned to `MI`.

## Strongest `PA` family: `KA-KU ~ KA-KU-PA`

Bare `KA-KU` is a complete quantified entry on HT 62. `KA-KU-PA` recurs on HT 16 and two Haghia Triada roundels (HT Wc 3015, 3016) in a tightly linked administrative cluster. The repeated extended form and same-site administrative concentration support common lexical identity, but the base/variant do not occupy an obviously identical role. The family is retained at **Tier B**.

## Two new v0.5 source failures

The hostile audit exposed two normalized/source conflicts that v0.5 did not remove.

### Apparent `JA-RE` base

Normalized ARKH1b tokenization supplies `JA-RE`, producing the automatic pair `JA-RE ~ JA-RE-MI`. The damage-preserving ARKH 1 transcription instead gives:

```text
]PA-RE
```

This changes both the first sign and the boundary state. The `MI` pair is rejected.

### Apparent `QA-*118-SA`

HT 70 actually preserves:

```text
]QA-*118-SA
```

The normalized corpus lost the insecure left boundary, so `QA-*118 ~ QA-*118-SA` is rejected.

These failures are frozen forward as source-consistency regression controls rather than rewriting the historical v0.5 run.

## `A-RI ~ A-RI-PA`

`A-RI` occurs twice on PH 6, an MM II word-only Phaistos tablet. `A-RI-PA` is a complete entry on PE 2 at Petras in a numerical/fractional account. The exact string relation survives, but the short base and site/documentary mismatch leave common lexical identity unproved. **Tier C**.

## `A-JE ~ A-JE-SA`

`A-JE` is a retrograde two-sign vessel inscription from Thera. `A-JE-SA` is transmitted from IO Za <1>, a limestone ladle inscription that is now lost and was not seen by GORILA. The formal comparison is retained only as **Tier C / source-limited**.

## Main conclusion

The screen does not add a second independently demonstrated productive suffix comparable to `JA`.

It does add two high-value local morphology anchors:

- **Tier A:** `I-DA ~ I-DA-MI`
- **Tier B:** `KA-KU ~ KA-KU-PA`

The next research phase should therefore stop descending the suffix ranking and move to **multi-ending stem grids**. `I-DA` is the first priority because v0.5 also contains `I-DA-A` and `I-DA-DA`, creating an internally generated three-ending family (`A`, `DA`, `MI`) around one secure stem. Those additional cells require independent hostile audit before any grammatical interpretation.
