# Lead: final `RA`

**Status:** audited; productivity not established.  
**v0.4 structural rank:** tied at three exact pairs behind `JA` and alongside `TI`/`TE` by pair count.  
**Manual outcome:** 0 Tier A + 1 Tier B + 1 Tier C + 1 rejected.

## Current defensible claim

> Final `RA` is not established as a productive general suffix by the present exact-pair evidence. One family, `MI-DA ~ MI-DA-RA`, remains a credible Tier-B formal candidate with possible onomastic/derivational character.

## Families

### `MI-DA ~ MI-DA-RA` — Tier B / reclassified

Both forms are complete. `MI-DA` occurs in an administrative recipient/designation-like slot on HT 27b; `MI-DA-RA` is a complete three-sign inscription on PK Zb 25. Schoep and Driessen describe the latter as a hapax of unknown meaning and cannot decide whether it is an anthroponym or toponym.

Keep as a possible onomastic/derivational relationship. Do not assign a case or translation.

### `A-DA ~ A-DA-RA` — Tier C / comparison-only

Complete exact string extension, but support and discourse environment diverge sharply: TY 3a administrative allocation versus KN Zf 31 silver hairpin. Common lexical identity is unproved.

The broader `A-DA` family remains interesting because `A-DA ~ A-DA-RO` independently survives as a Tier-B `RO` candidate. This should motivate a future multi-ending stem-family test rather than promotion of `RA` by itself.

### `SA-RA ~ SA-RA-RA` — rejected as `-RA` evidence

The automatic decomposition is superseded by the stronger same-tablet comparison on HT 30: `SA-RA₂ ~ SA-RA-RA`. Younger explicitly proposes those two forms may be one and the same. The source therefore does not support treating `SA-RA-RA` as straightforward `SA-RA + RA`.

Retain as a string-decomposition ambiguity control.

## Functional status

- a productive suffix inferred from `SA-RA-RA` alone.
