# Lead: final `NE`

**Status:** one Tier-A local paradigm; productive suffixhood across multiple independent stems not established.  
**Meaning/function:** unknown.

## Current claim

The secure family is:

```text
*21F-TU      ~ *21F-TU-NE
```

At Haghia Triada, HT Scribe 9 writes both forms on separate tablets, and Scribe 11 independently replicates the extended form. This is strong sign-level evidence that final `NE` is morphological in this lexical family.

However, a full audit of the four original automatic `NE` pairs leaves only this one family as credible morphology.

## `*21F-TU` family

### HT 94b — bare

- Haghia Triada
- HT Scribe 9
- complete `*21F-TU`
- numbered administrative entry
- secondary transaction model: recipient-like

### HT 87 — extended

- Haghia Triada
- HT Scribe 9
- complete `*21F-TU-NE`
- initial heading/frame position before `MA-KA-RI-TE` and a one-unit list
- secondary transaction model: sender/header-like

### HT 7b — extended replication

- Haghia Triada
- HT Scribe 11
- complete `*21F-TU-NE`
- numbered list entry followed by `1`
- secondary transaction model: recipient-like

This combination is unusually strong formally because it defeats a simple scribal-orthography explanation while also showing that the extended form is not restricted to one local syntactic role.

## Why productivity is not yet established

The other apparent `NE` pairs fail independently:

### `QE-TU`

HT 41 preserves only `]QE-TU`. The complete `QE-TU-NE` on HT 12 therefore cannot be paired with a secure free base.

### `PA-TA`

KH 79+89 explicitly preserves `]PA-TA`. Complete `PA-TA-NE` forms elsewhere are real, but the bare-word side of the paradigm is missing.

### `PA-RA`

Both `PA-RA` and `PA-RA-NE` are complete, and both can appear in recipient-like administrative contexts. But the Haghia Triada forms are segregated by scribe, and `PA-RA-NE` is independently recognized in Linear A/Linear B comparative work as a Minoan personal name. Common lexical identity with bare `PA-RA` is therefore not secure enough to count as a productive suffix paradigm.

## Functional stop

The one Tier-A family does **not** yield a stable meaning for `NE`:

```text
HT94b   *21F-TU       recipient-like
HT87    *21F-TU-NE    header/sender-like
HT7b    *21F-TU-NE    recipient-like
```

So do not gloss `NE` as:

- recipient/dative;
- sender/source;
- genitive;
- heading marker;
- plural;
- locative;
- any specific case.

The secondary transaction labels are contextual aids, not grammar.

## Comparative position

After equivalent manual auditing:

- **`JA`** — productive morphology supported by 2 Tier-A + 2 Tier-B independent families;
- **`NE`** — 1 Tier-A family; productivity unproved;
- **`ME`** — 1 Tier-B family; productivity unproved.

This makes `JA` structurally exceptional among the non-Davis suffix candidates tested so far.

## Next test

Do not spend another function experiment on `NE` until a second secure independent `X ~ X-NE` family appears.

The higher-value next step is comparative corpus engineering:

1. add the `QE-TU` and `PA-TA` damaged bases as permanent regression controls;
2. add `PA-RA ~ PA-RA-NE` as an onomastic/lexical-identity negative control;
3. build v0.4 type-aware extraction to catch cross-face continuation and complex logogram flattening;
4. audit the next suffix family only after v0.4 reranking.

The project retains `*21F` rather than assigning `QI` as a phonetic value in its formal benchmark. No translation or language-family inference follows from this lead.
