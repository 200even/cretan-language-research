# Lead: `DA-KU-` and possible final `-TI`

**Status:** strongest current grammatical lead  
**Lead type:** internal Linear A morphology  
**Proposed function of `-TI`:** unresolved; locative/origin/relational function is a testable hypothesis only

## Internal family

### HT 103 — Scribe 3

The tablet contains a tightly controlled same-scribe contrast:

- `DA-KU-SE-NE 6 1/2`
- `DA-KU-NA 1`
- `DA-KU-SE-NE 1`

All are securely segmented and occur in the same administrative list architecture.

Raw inscription: https://lineara.xyz/items/HT103.html

This establishes a recurring `DA-KU-` base with at least two longer formations:

- `DA-KU-NA`
- `DA-KU-SE-NE`

It does **not** by itself prove where every morpheme boundary falls.

### SE Zf 1 — bare `DA-KU`

A bronze double axe from Selakanos bears only:

- `DA-KU`

Raw inscription: https://lineara.xyz/items/SEZf1.html

Because this is a different site, object type, and genre with no known scribe or administrative syntax, it is useful only as evidence that `DA-KU` can stand as a complete sign group. It is not treated as a direct paradigm member for grammatical-function assignment.

## Cross-scribe `-TI` alternation

### HT 104 — Scribe 5

The raw transcription reads:

- `DA-KU-SE-NE-TI 45 1/2`
- `I-DU-TI 20 1/2`
- `PA-DA-SU-TI 29`
- `KU-RO 95`

Raw inscription: https://lineara.xyz/items/HT104.html

The three entry quantities sum exactly:

`45.5 + 20.5 + 29 = 95`

This makes the three `...-TI` words strongly parallel within the accounting structure.

The crucial cross-scribe comparison is:

- Scribe 3, HT 103: `DA-KU-SE-NE`
- Scribe 5, HT 104: `DA-KU-SE-NE-TI`

That is a cleaner candidate for productive grammatical morphology than the earlier generalized N-series hypothesis.

## Data-layer correction

A derived transaction model for HT 104 represented the first word as `DA-KU-SE-NE`, silently omitting final `TI`. The raw sign-by-sign transcription preserves `TI`.

For this project, the raw transcription is authoritative. This correction is important because it **creates**, rather than destroys, the strongest current morphological lead.

## Minimal current claim

There is good internal evidence for a formal contrast:

`DA-KU-SE-NE-Ø` ~ `DA-KU-SE-NE-TI`

and HT 104 independently places final `TI` on three parallel accounting entries.

Therefore:

> final `TI` is a credible candidate for productive Linear A grammatical morphology.

## Possible function

An earlier line of scholarship has considered `-te/-ti` in locative/postpositional constructions. Separately, recent statistical work on Linear A morphology supports the existence of strongly distributed final grammatical signs.

Those facts motivate, but do not prove, a working hypothesis that `-TI` may express something in the semantic space of:

- location (`at/in X`),
- origin/source (`from X`),
- relational/toponymic association (`X-associated`).

No one of those values is adopted as a translation here.

## Why this lead is strong

1. Secure raw word boundaries.
2. Multi-sign shared base.
3. Cross-scribe contrast.
4. Same-site administrative context.
5. Three independent `-TI` forms in the same syntactic/numerical structure on HT 104.
6. The proposed pattern was discovered through correction of a derived-data error, not by searching for a desired Greek cognate.

## Critical next test

Run a corpus-wide strict search for:

- `X` ~ `X-TI`
- `X` ~ `X-TE`

requiring:

- multi-sign stems;
- secure word boundaries;
- cross-scribe replication where possible;
- equivalent syntactic slots;
- site/scribe/genre controls;
- raw-transcription verification.

If `-TI` repeatedly attaches to forms that occupy location/source/relational slots while bare forms occupy another role, a grammatical function may become assignable without deciphering the lexical stems.

## Falsifiers

Downgrade this lead if:

- `DA-KU-SE-NE` and `DA-KU-SE-NE-TI` prove to be unrelated lexical items;
- `-TI` is shown to be part of stems rather than a separable ending across the broader corpus;
- the apparent parallelism on HT 104 reflects a non-grammatical scribal convention;
- corpus-wide testing shows no additional controlled X/X-TI alternations.
