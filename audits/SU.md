# Audit: final `SU`

**Status:** completed comparative audit.  
**Input:** two structurally accepted v0.4 exact pairs.  
**Result:** **no Tier-A/B family survives; productive final `SU` is not established.**

## Registered pairs

1. `KU-NI ~ KU-NI-SU`
2. `A-RI ~ A-RI-SU`

## 1. `KU-NI ~ KU-NI-SU`

**Final adjudication: rejected as a secure exact suffix paradigm because the base boundary is source-conflicted/insecure.**

The normalized HT 79+83 item page parses `KU-NI` as a complete two-sign word. However, the separate GORILA-derived commentary for HT 79 [+] 83 preserves the same line as:

```text
]KU-NI[
```

Both external boundaries are therefore uncertain in the higher-fidelity damage-preserving layer. Under the project's evidence hierarchy, the normalized word segmentation cannot override the boundary-preserving transcription.

This also corrects the project's earlier treatment of `KU-NI ~ KU-NI-TE`: the claim that HT 79+83 supplied a secure complete `KU-NI` was too strong.

`KU-NI-SU` itself is securely and repeatedly attested at Haghia Triada, including HT 10, HT 86, and HT 95. The forms occur in administrative list/header contexts. Published scholarship has long treated `KU-NI-SU` as a lexical unit, variously proposing a place-name or grain-related interpretation; none of those proposals licenses segmentation as `KU-NI + SU`.

Thus the extended form is real, but the present corpus does not supply a secure whole-word `KU-NI` base for this exact-pair test.

## 2. `A-RI ~ A-RI-SU`

**Final adjudication: Tier C / comparison-only.**

Complete `A-RI` occurs twice on PH 6, a Middle Minoan II Phaistos tablet consisting almost entirely of unnumbered word sequences. Younger's commentary notes that the document may contain place-related forms and treats repeated `A-RI` as a lexical/tag-like item.

Complete `A-RI-SU` occurs on HT 118, a Late Minoan IB Haghia Triada administrative tablet of specialized commodities, where it is a numbered entry.

Both strings are epigraphically secure, but the comparison crosses:

- site: Phaistos → Haghia Triada;
- chronology: MM II → LM IB;
- document structure: word-only list → quantified administrative account;
- unknown lexical class/function.

No same-scribe, same-tablet, or repeated intermediate evidence links them. The exact string relationship is therefore retained only as a future lexical comparison.
