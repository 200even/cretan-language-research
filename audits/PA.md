## 1. `KA-KU ~ KA-KU-PA` — Tier B formal candidate

Both strings are securely attested.

### Bare `KA-KU`

HT 62, the large fragment of HT 62 [+] 73, preserves complete `KA-KU` as a quantified entry:

```text
KA-KU   4[
```

The tablet is a Haghia Triada single-commodity account assigned to HT Scribe 23.

### Extended `KA-KU-PA`

The extension recurs independently at Haghia Triada:

- HT 16.1-2: `KA-KU-PA • DI-NA-U ...`, mixed-commodity page tablet, HT Scribe 10;
- HT Wc 3015: roundel statement `KA-KU-PA`, *303, three impressions;
- HT Wc 3016: roundel statement `KA-KU-PA`, *304+PA / *303+D, two impressions.

Published contextual work on Haghia Triada administrative shipment/account groups independently treats `KA-KU-PA` as a recurring heading in the tightly linked HT 16 / roundel cluster. This supports lexical reality of the extended form but does not tell us what final `PA` means.

### Assessment

The same site, repeated extended form, independent scribal/support attestations, and administrative concentration make common lexical identity plausible. However, the bare form is a quantified entry while the extended form behaves prominently as a heading/roundel statement; there is no same-tablet or same-scribe alternation demonstrating ordinary inflection.

**Adjudication:** **Tier B formal candidate**. Possible derivational/designational morphology; function unknown.

## 2. `A-RI ~ A-RI-PA` — Tier C comparison-only

### Bare `A-RI`

PH 6 (Phaistos, MM II context) is an unusual word-only tablet with no numbers. `A-RI` occurs twice, on lines .1 and .3. Younger's commentary notes the duplication and possible relation to final `-A-RI` inside the longer line-.4 group, but assigns no secure meaning.

### Extended `A-RI-PA`

PE 2 at Petras preserves complete `A-RI-PA` in a numerical/fractional account from a mixed deposit.

### Assessment

The string extension is exact and both forms are complete, but the comparison crosses site, documentary structure, and likely chronology. `A-RI` is also a very short base. No repeated contextual slot establishes that the two are the same lexical item rather than coincidentally related sign groups.

**Adjudication:** **Tier C / comparison-only**.
