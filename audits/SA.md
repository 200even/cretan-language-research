## Frozen pairs

1. `QA-*118 ~ QA-*118-SA`

## 1. `QA-*118 ~ QA-*118-SA` — rejected: variant is left-fragmentary

The bare form `QA-*118` is securely attested on KH 10.3 in a Khania administrative tablet; the source itself cross-references prefixed `I-QA-*118` at Haghia Triada.

The apparent `QA-*118-SA` extension in normalized v0.5 data comes from HT 70. Direct inspection of the damage-preserving transcription gives:

```text
]QA-*118-SA
```

The left boundary is therefore insecure. A complete `QA-*118-SA` word is not established.

**Adjudication:** rejected as an exact suffix paradigm.

This is a newly discovered v0.5 source-mask miss and should become a forward regression control.

## 2. `A-JE ~ A-JE-SA` — Tier C / source-limited comparison

### Bare `A-JE`

THE Zb 7 is a vessel fragment from Thera containing only:

```text
A-JE
```

written retrograde. The form is complete as transmitted.

### Extended `A-JE-SA`

IO Za <1> is a limestone ladle from Iouktas. The normalized item gives the three-sign word `A-JE-SA`, but the source history is unusually weak: Younger's commentary states that the painted inscription was **not seen by GORILA** and is now lost; the reading ultimately depends on the older published record.

### Assessment

Both transmitted strings are formally compatible with final `SA`, and both occur on vessels rather than ordinary administrative tablets. But they are isolated cross-site forms, the base is only two signs, and the extended inscription can no longer be independently rechecked against the object or GORILA facsimile.

**Adjudication:** **Tier C / comparison-only, source-limited**.
