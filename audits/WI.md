# Audit: final `WI`

**Status:** completed comparative audit.  
**Input:** two structurally accepted v0.4 exact pairs.  
**Result:** **one Tier-B cross-register morphological candidate survives; productive final `WI` is not established.**

## Registered pairs

1. `JA-DI ~ JA-DI-WI`
2. `PA3-NI ~ PA3-NI-WI`

## 1. `JA-DI ~ JA-DI-WI`

**Final adjudication: rejected as a secure exact suffix pair.**

`JA-DI` is complete on PH Wc 39, a Phaistos roundel from an MM II context.

The normalized corpus represented KN Zb 35 as complete `JA-DI-WI`. The GORILA-derived commentary instead preserves the pithos inscription as three damaged segments:

```text
]JA[    ]DI-[    ]WI[
```

The sequence is therefore not a securely segmented three-sign word. v0.4 manufactured an exact pair by flattening separated damaged segments into one normalized form.

This is a new structural regression control: **multi-fragment word reconstruction / segmented-fragment flattening**.

## 2. `PA3-NI ~ PA3-NI-WI`

**Final adjudication: Tier B / reclassified morphological candidate.**

The base is securely and repeatedly attested in Haghia Triada administrative material:

- HT 85a, Scribe 9: `PA3-NI 12` in a personnel/account context;
- HT 102, Scribe 5: another complete `PA3-NI` administrative entry.

Younger's analysis of HT 85a treats the recurring names on side a, including `PA3-NI`, as probable toponyms or regional designations. That is an interpretation, not a translation, but it provides a plausible lexical-class warning.

`PA3-NI-WI` is complete on SY Za 4, a serpentine libation table. It occurs as the third lexical sequence in the libation formula:

```text
A-TA-I-*301-WA-JA • JA-I-NWA-ZA • PA3-NI-WI
```

Rose Thomas's analysis of the libation formula notes that `PA3-NI-WI` occupies the slot more commonly filled by `JA-/A-SA-SA-RA-ME`. This makes the variant a genuine ritual lexical/formula element, not an ordinary administrative entry.

Why it remains Tier B rather than C:

- repeated complete base attestations;
- complete exact extended form;
- no damage or type problem;
- `PA3-NI` also participates in other longer forms such as `PA3-NI-NA`, making a stem-plus-ending analysis structurally plausible.

Why it is not Tier A:

- site/support/register mismatch;
- likely toponymic/designation character of the base;
- ritual-formula specialization of the extended form;
- no same-scribe or same-document alternation.

The safest interpretation is **possible onomastic/relational/derivational morphology**, with function unknown.
