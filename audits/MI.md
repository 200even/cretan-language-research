# Audit: final `-MI`

**Status:** completed under the preregistered `MI/PA/SA` comparative protocol.  
**Source-consistent v0.5 exact-paradigm rank:** suffix **#3**, two apparent secure pairs.  
**Final result:** **1 Tier A + 1 rejected. Productive final `MI` is not established under the frozen two-family rule.**

## Frozen pairs

1. `JA-RE ~ JA-RE-MI`
2. `I-DA ~ I-DA-MI`

## 1. `JA-RE ~ JA-RE-MI` — rejected: normalized/source reading conflict

`JA-RE-MI` is securely written on HT 87.3, a Haghia Triada page tablet assigned to HT Scribe 9. Younger's commentary describes HT 87 as probably a list of people by name; `JA-RE-MI` is a numbered entry.

The apparent bare base came from normalized ARKH1b tokenization as `JA-RE`. Direct inspection of the damage-preserving ARKH 1 commentary overturns that reading for morphology purposes. ARKH 1b.1 preserves:

```text
]PA-RE   3[
```

not a securely bounded `JA-RE`.

Thus the computational pair was manufactured by a disagreement between normalized item tokenization and the higher-fidelity inscription transcription: the first sign differs (`JA` vs `PA`) and the left boundary is insecure.

**Adjudication:** rejected as an exact `X ~ X-MI` paradigm.

**New failure class/control:** normalized sign-reading divergence plus lost fragment boundary. Register for the next source-consistency regression set.

## 2. `I-DA ~ I-DA-MI` — Tier A formal morphology

This is the strong result of the audit.

### Bare `I-DA`

PK Za 18 is a serpentine libation table. Its preserved sequence includes:

```text
]-TE • I-DA • JA-JA-[
```

`I-DA` itself is complete and bounded by dividers.

A second complete bare `I-DA` occurs on ZA 24a in an administrative tablet before a grain logogram, demonstrating that the spelling is not unique to one ritual object.

### Extended `I-DA-MI`

SY Za 1 is another libation table. It preserves:

```text
]A-TA-I-*301-WA-JA • I-DA-MI • JA-•-[
```

The extended form is complete and bounded.

### Why this is stronger than edit distance

The critical comparison is PK Za 18 vs SY Za 1:

```text
• I-DA    • JA-...
• I-DA-MI • JA-...
```

The two forms occur on the same support class, in the same ritual/libation genre, in an extremely similar local formulaic slot, independently at Palaikastro and Syme. This materially strengthens common lexical identity.

**Adjudication:** **Tier A formal morphology**.

No meaning is assigned to `MI`. The result establishes a credible final extension in this family, not whether it is inflectional, derivational, clitic-like, or lexically restricted.

Those additional endings require their own hostile audit before grammatical interpretation, but together they make `I-DA` an unusually valuable Kober-style grid anchor.
