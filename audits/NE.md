# Audit: final `NE`

**Status:** completed first-pass family audit.  
**Input:** four v0.2 `X ~ X-NE` candidates, re-evaluated against damage-aware v0.3 and inscription/context evidence.  
**Result:** **one Tier-A morphological family survives; productive final `NE` across multiple independent stems is not established.**

## Audit question

Does final `NE` behave as a productive suffix across more than one securely attested lexical base, or is the signal driven by one strong family plus damaged/onomastic false comparisons?

The four registered families were:

1. `*21F-TU ~ *21F-TU-NE`
2. `QE-TU ~ QE-TU-NE`
3. `PA-TA ~ PA-TA-NE`
4. `PA-RA ~ PA-RA-NE`

v0.3 already reduced these to two damage-secure string pairs:

- `*21F-TU ~ *21F-TU-NE`
- `PA-RA ~ PA-RA-NE`

The other two are retained as negative controls.

## 1. `*21F-TU ~ *21F-TU-NE`

**Final adjudication: Tier A positive.**

### Bare form

HT 94b, Haghia Triada, HT Scribe 9:

```text
*21F-TU • 1
```

The form is complete and occurs as a numbered administrative/list entry. The upstream secondary transaction model labels it recipient-like.

### Extended forms

HT 87, Haghia Triada, HT Scribe 9:

```text
*21F-TU-NE • MA-KA-RI-TE • ...
```

The same scribe who writes bare `*21F-TU` writes complete `*21F-TU-NE`. On HT 87 the form occupies an initial heading/frame position in a one-unit list; the upstream secondary transaction model treats it as sender/header-like.

HT 7b, Haghia Triada, HT Scribe 11:

```text
*21F-TU-NE 1
DA-RU-*329 2
```

This independently replicates the extended form under another scribe. Here the upstream model treats `*21F-TU-NE` as a recipient/list entry.

### Why it survives

- complete base and extended forms;
- same site and administrative register;
- **same-scribe base/extended contrast** under Scribe 9;
- extended form independently replicated by Scribe 11;
- no sign substitution or reconstructed material required.

The changing derived transaction roles prevent a simple functional gloss. In particular, `NE` cannot presently be equated with recipient, sender, heading, or a simple case role.

This remains one of the strongest exact morphology controls in the benchmark.

## 2. `QE-TU ~ QE-TU-NE`

**Final adjudication: rejected as an exact suffix paradigm.**

HT 41 preserves the supposed base as:

```text
]QE-TU 10
```

The left boundary is broken. v0.3 correctly excludes the base as insecure.

HT 12 contains complete:

```text
QE-TU-NE • QA2+[?]+PU 1
```

The extended form is real, but a complete `QE-TU` whole word is not established by the current evidence. Therefore this cannot support `NE` morphology mechanically.

The lexical resemblance remains a future comparison if an independent complete `QE-TU` attestation is identified.

## 3. `PA-TA ~ PA-TA-NE`

**Final adjudication: rejected as an exact suffix paradigm.**

The sole computational base comes from KH 79+89. The inscription commentary explicitly gives:

```text
]PA-TA • A-RA-[
```

so the left boundary of the apparent base is insecure. v0.3 correctly removes it.

By contrast, `PA-TA-NE` is securely attested in Haghia Triada administrative material, including HT 94b, where it is a numbered one-unit entry. The existence of a complete extended form cannot repair the missing boundary of the proposed base.

## 4. `PA-RA ~ PA-RA-NE`

**Final adjudication: reclassified / rejected as evidence for productive `NE`; retain as a lexical-identity control.**

Both strings are epigraphically secure.

### Bare `PA-RA`

Secure examples include:

- HT 128a, Haghia Triada, HT Scribe 9;
- PH 3a, Phaistos.

On HT 128a the upstream transaction model treats `PA-RA` as a recipient-like participant governing several grain allocations.

### `PA-RA-NE`

Secure examples include:

- HT 115a, Haghia Triada, HT Scribe 8;
- HT 115b, Haghia Triada, HT Scribe 8.

HT 115a likewise places `PA-RA-NE` in a recipient-like numbered list slot.

This superficial contextual equivalence initially makes the pair attractive. However two independent problems remain:

1. **scribal segregation:** the Haghia Triada bare and extended forms are written by different scribes, while `PA-RA-NE` is restricted in the present HT sample to Scribe 8;
2. **onomastic evidence:** comparative Linear A/Linear B scholarship treats `pa-ra-ne` as a Minoan personal name attested across the Linear tradition.

Thus the exact string relationship does not establish that `PA-RA-NE` is a grammatical `PA-RA + NE` derivative. The base may be unrelated, or the relation may be onomastic/lexical rather than productive inflection.

The pair remains useful as a negative lexical-identity control because a purely computational method will continue to find it.

## Summary

| family | v0.3 status | final result |
|---|---|---|
| `*21F-TU ~ *21F-TU-NE` | secure | **Tier A positive** |
| `QE-TU ~ QE-TU-NE` | base insecure | **rejected** |
| `PA-TA ~ PA-TA-NE` | base insecure | **rejected** |
| `PA-RA ~ PA-RA-NE` | secure | **reclassified / lexical-identity control** |

### Survival rate

- v0.2 apparent pairs: **4**
- v0.3 damage-secure pairs: **2**
- manually credible morphology families: **1**
- Tier-A families: **1**

Therefore:

> **Final `NE` has one strong local morphological paradigm, but current exact-pair evidence does not establish that it is productive across independent stems.**

This profile is much weaker than audited `JA` productivity, but stronger in local evidence quality than `ME`, whose sole survivor is only Tier B.

## Functional result

The `*21F-TU` family does not yield a stable grammatical function:

- bare `*21F-TU` on HT 94b is recipient-like in the secondary model;
- `*21F-TU-NE` on HT 87 is header/sender-like;
- `*21F-TU-NE` on HT 7b is recipient-like.

Therefore no assignment such as dative, genitive, sender/source, recipient, or heading marker is promoted.

## Comparative implication

The audited suffix profiles now differ sharply:

- `JA`: multiple independent Tier-A/B families survive; productive morphology supported;
- `NE`: one Tier-A family survives; productivity not yet demonstrated;
- `ME`: only one Tier-B family survives; productivity not demonstrated;
- Davis suffixes: mixed distributional/paradigmatic profiles documented separately.

This supports maintaining **family survival** and **corpus-wide boundary distribution** as independent evidence dimensions.

## Non-claim

This audit does not assign a phonetic value to `*21F`, translate `NE`, or establish a Minoan noun case. It establishes one robust sign-level base/extended relationship and rejects the claim that the present four-pair sample demonstrates productive `NE` across the corpus.
