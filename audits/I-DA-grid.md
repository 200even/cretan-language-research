# Multi-ending audit: `I-DA`

**Status:** completed first anchor-cell audit under `experiments/multi-ending-grid-v01.md`.  
**Purpose:** determine whether the v0.5 forms `I-DA-A`, `I-DA-DA`, and `I-DA-MI` constitute a source-secure multi-ending family before searching for a second stem with overlapping endings.

## Baseline stem

Complete bare `I-DA` occurs at least in:

- PK Za 18, a serpentine libation table: `]-TE • I-DA • JA-JA-[`;
- ZA 24a, an administrative tablet: `I-DA` before a damaged grain logogram.

The ritual PK Za 18 attestation is the primary contextual comparator for the extended ritual forms.

## Cell 1: `I-DA-MI` — Tier A

Already established by the completed `MI` audit.

SY Za 1, a libation table, has complete `I-DA-MI` between dividers in a closely comparable ritual formula environment. The PK Za 18 / SY Za 1 comparison is cross-site and same-genre, with `I-DA` and `I-DA-MI` occupying closely analogous divider-bounded positions.

**Adjudication:** **Tier A formal morphology**. Function unknown.

## Cell 2: `I-DA-A` — Tier B

KO Za 1 is a stone base carrying a substantial libation-formula inscription. It preserves complete:

```text
... DU-PU3-RE • I-DA-A • U-NA-KA-NA-SI ...
```

The form is securely bounded and belongs to the same broad ritual/libation system as the complete bare `I-DA` on PK Za 18 and `I-DA-MI` on SY Za 1.

The contextual convergence makes common stem identity plausible. However, the immediate right neighbor differs from the fragmentary PK Za 18 sequence, and there is no same-object alternation proving that final `A` and final `MI` occupy the same grammatical slot.

**Adjudication:** **Tier B formal candidate**.

## Cell 3: `I-DA-DA` — Tier C

CR Zg 4 is a steatite reel seal read from its impression. One face/section carries complete:

```text
I-DA-DA
```

The reading is secure and is reported as confirmed in later specialist discussion, but the support and documentary function differ radically from the libation-table family. No local context links `I-DA-DA` to the same ritual lexical item.

**Adjudication:** **Tier C / comparison-only**.

## Result

| stem | ending | form | tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| `I-DA` | `MI` | `I-DA-MI` | **A** |
| `I-DA` | `A` | `I-DA-A` | **B** |
| `I-DA` | `DA` | `I-DA-DA` | **C** |

This establishes `I-DA` as a **credible multi-ending family**, because at least two independent endings (`MI`, `A`) survive at Tier A/B. It does **not** establish a grammatical paradigm: the preregistered grid rule still requires a second independent stem sharing at least two endings.
