# Kato Syme / Ephorus Hostile Review and Disposition

**Review status:** APPROVED FOR EXECUTION after revision  
**Recorded:** 2026-08-20

## Original methodological problem

The initial proposal attempted to connect site-enriched Linear A vocabulary at Kato Syme with later Cretan institutions described by Ephorus and other Greek sources. The extraction procedure was candidate-blind, but the proposed semantic interpretation was not.

The hostile review identified three fatal traps:

1. **Comparison-site bias:** privileging Palaikastro because of later kouros/youth associations would make a shared anomaly circular evidence for the desired historical interpretation.
2. **Toponym/theonym default:** a site-enriched sequence could easily be a personal name, place name, deity name, title, or other referential entity rather than specialized ritual/institutional vocabulary.
3. **Continuity fallacy:** continuity of sacred space across the Bronze/Iron transition does not establish continuity of cult, vocabulary, theology, or institution.

The reviewer required orthogonal sanctuary controls, a programmatic proper-name compatibility screen, strict chronological stratification, and removal of Ephorus from the evidentiary chain.

## Disposition

| Referee objection | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Semantic circularity | **ACCEPTED.** Later Greek evidence is excluded from candidate meaning and primary interpretation. |
| Palaikastro selection bias | **ACCEPTED.** Palaikastro has no privileged role; site similarity is generated blind from Linear A features. |
| Sanctuary continuity = linguistic continuity | **REJECTED as an inference.** Only spatial continuity may be stated without independent bridging evidence. |
| Toponym/theonym null | **ACCEPTED.** PN-0 to PN-3 proper-name compatibility screen is mandatory. |
| Dummy/orthogonal sanctuary controls | **IMPLEMENTED.** Peak/rural sanctuary, formula-matched, settlement/palatial ritual, and administrative controls are defined. |
| Chronological confounding | **IMPLEMENTED.** MM III/LM I and uncertain material remain stratified. |
| Repeated-token inflation | **IMPLEMENTED.** Primary unit is independent inscription/object. |
| Formula-slot confounding | **IMPLEMENTED.** Local formula variants are distinguished from independent lexical material. |

## Semantic firewall

A positive Kato Syme result may establish only that a textual feature is unusually associated with Kato Syme after the frozen controls. It cannot establish the meanings "youth," "initiation," "hunting," "andreion," "agela," or any later Greek institutional concept.

Ephorus is retained only as historical motivation for why Kato Syme drew attention. He is not an evidentiary input to the Linear A analysis.

## Final referee verdict

The revised architecture was approved for execution as a **standalone epigraphic branch**. The reviewer specifically endorsed:

- candidate-blind unsupervised site similarity;
- the PN-0 to PN-3 proper-name compatibility screen;
- the spatial-versus-linguistic continuity distinction;
- inscription-level counting;
- stratified permutation robustness;
- formula-position control.

## Sparsity warning

The reviewer additionally emphasized that the small and fragmentary SY Za corpus may make the preregistered OR/FDR/recurrence thresholds effectively underpowered. A failure caused by insufficient recurrence must therefore be represented as `INDETERMINATE_DUE_TO_SPARSITY`, not automatically as a linguistic negative.

A separately preregistered pan-Cretan rare-register contingency may activate only after such an indeterminate outcome. It does not relax Phase A thresholds and may not use Phase A candidate identities to define its candidate universe.
