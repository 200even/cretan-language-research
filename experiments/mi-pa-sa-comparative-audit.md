# Comparative hostile audit: final `MI`, `PA`, and `SA`

**Status:** registered before inscription-level source/context adjudication.  
**Discovery substrate:** source-consistent v0.5.  
**Purpose:** test three tied two-family suffix candidates under one common promotion rule, without selecting the most attractive family after inspection.

## Frozen candidate set

### `MI`
1. `JA-RE ~ JA-RE-MI`
2. `I-DA ~ I-DA-MI`

### `PA`
1. `KA-KU ~ KA-KU-PA`
2. `A-RI ~ A-RI-PA`

### `SA`
1. `QA-*118 ~ QA-*118-SA`
2. `A-JE ~ A-JE-SA`

These are the highest-ranked previously unaudited v0.5 suffixes with two structurally retained exact pairs each.

## Frozen adjudication protocol

Each pair is checked for:

1. authoritative/latest transcription and source hierarchy;
2. secure left/right word boundaries and damage state;
3. composite-object / line / face continuation;
4. complex-sign, logogram, ligature, or cross-script contamination;
5. same lexeme plausibility rather than substring coincidence;
6. site, chronology, support, scribe, and documentary genre;
7. immediate numerical/logographic and list-frame context;
8. onomastic, toponymic, ritual-formula, or other lexical-class concentration;
9. competing inscription-internal decompositions;
10. independent replication across stems.

## Tier rules

- **Tier A:** complete forms plus strong local/common-lexeme evidence, preferably same tablet/scribe or independently replicated comparable context.
- **Tier B:** complete formal relationship with credible common lexical identity but unresolved derivational/onomastic/contextual function.
- **Tier C:** complete string comparison only; common lexical identity remains weak.
- **Reclassified:** real forms but a stronger alternative analysis supersedes generic suffixation.
- **Rejected:** source, boundary, segmentation, type, or lexical evidence defeats the exact-pair claim.

## Frozen productivity criterion

A suffix is promoted as **productive morphology** only if **both independent frozen stem families survive at Tier A or Tier B**.

A single strong family is retained as local morphology evidence but is insufficient for productivity.

No grammatical function or translation is assigned from pair survival alone.

## Stop rule

If a suffix meets the productivity criterion, the next experiment tests function across its surviving families using the same staged hierarchy previously applied to `JA`: coarse context, literal micro-syntax, then broader list/frame relations. If those tests fail, function remains unknown rather than being inferred from external language resemblance.
