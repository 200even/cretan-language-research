# Multi-ending stem-grid experiment v0.1

**Status:** preregistered after completion of the MI/PA/SA comparative suffix audit and before source adjudication of the new grid cells.  
**Discovery substrate:** frozen source-consistent v0.5 exact-pair output plus completed manual audits.  
**Goal:** move from one-affix-at-a-time discovery toward Kober-style recurring stem × ending structure without assigning meanings.

## Primary anchor: `I-DA`

The completed MI audit establishes:

- `I-DA ~ I-DA-MI` — **Tier A**.

The frozen v0.5 structural output independently contains:

- `I-DA ~ I-DA-A`;
- `I-DA ~ I-DA-DA`.

These two additional cells are **not promoted by preregistration**. They must undergo the same hostile source/context audit as every previous morphology claim.

## Stage 1: anchor-cell audit

Audit the three extensions:

| stem | ending | form | current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| `I-DA` | `MI` | `I-DA-MI` | Tier A from completed MI audit |
| `I-DA` | `A` | `I-DA-A` | structural candidate only |
| `I-DA` | `DA` | `I-DA-DA` | structural candidate only |

For `A` and `DA`, record source integrity, support/genre, local neighbors, site/chronology, formula position, and competing segmentation.

## Stage 2: corpus-wide ending matrix

Using the frozen v0.5 exact-pair output, build a matrix where:

- rows = base forms;
- columns = final one-sign extensions;
- cells = source-consistent exact `X ~ X-S` candidates;
- manually rejected/source-conflicted cells are masked;
- Tier A/B/C and lexical warnings remain separate annotations.

No composite morphology score is introduced.

## Stage 3: grid-promotion rule

A **paradigm grid** requires more than one multi-ending stem.

Promotion requires either:

1. at least **two independent stems sharing at least two of the same endings**, with all four cells source-secure and at least three cells Tier A/B; or
2. one three-ending stem plus a second independent stem reproducing at least two of those endings, again with source-secure cells and no stronger competing decomposition.

A single stem with three endings is called a **multi-ending family**, not a grammatical paradigm.

## Functional inference rule

Even a promoted grid does not identify case, number, tense, person, gender, or any other grammatical category. Function is tested only after the formal grid is established and must predict recurring inscriptional context across independent stems.

## Negative controls

The matrix must preserve already rejected cells, including source-conflicted or decomposition-failed candidates, so apparent grid density cannot be inflated by known false positives.

## Immediate questions

1. Do `I-DA-A` and `I-DA-DA` survive direct source audit?
2. Do `A`, `DA`, and `MI` recur as endings on any second secure stem?
3. Which already audited stems (`A-DA`, `MI-DA`, `KU-PA`, `A-RI`, etc.) produce the strongest overlapping ending sets after manual adjudication?
4. Does a genuine 2×2 or larger grid emerge without semantic assumptions?

## Non-claim

This experiment is structural. It does not translate `I-DA` or any ending and does not identify the Minoan language.
